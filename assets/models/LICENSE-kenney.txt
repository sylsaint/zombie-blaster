Kenney assets used as the base for the v3 models in this folder.
The v3 glbs are re-modelled part by part on the proportions/part layout of:
  - Kenney Mini Characters 1.0 (character-male-c) -> chr_soldier_a/b/c  (wpn_*.glb are our own v2 gun shapes, re-posed, not Kenney-derived)
  - Kenney Graveyard Kit 5.0 (character-zombie)   -> enm_walker(_lod1), enm_runner(_lod1), enm_elite_brute (style only)
